Where the ERP fails — and what an agent does instead
Scenario: customer orders 47 cases, layer = 10 Broken layer
ERP behavior
1
Checks MOQ (min 10 cases). 47 ≥ 10 → passes.
2
No layer/pallet validation exists. Order accepted as-is.
3
Warehouse receives pick ticket for 47. Worker must break open layer 5, remove 3 cases, re-wrap.
4
3 loose cases go to overstock. 12–18% labor premium applied retroactively — or absorbed as cost.
ASOE agent behavior
1
Reads pallet config: Ti=10, Hi=5 → layer=10, pallet=50.
2
Detects 47 mod 10 = 7. Broken layer — 3 cases short of full layer 5.
3
Checks customer exception table: no broken-layer waiver on file.
4
Generates two alternatives:
Option A: Round up to 50 (+$44.40)
Option B: Round down to 40 (−$103.60)
5
Routes to CSR with pre-drafted customer email showing cost delta for each option.
Scenario: cross-line pallet optimization ERP blind spot
ERP behavior
1
Validates each line independently. Line 1: 23 cases of SKU-A (layer=10). Line 2: 17 cases of SKU-B (layer=10).
2
Both lines pass MOQ. Both are broken layers. Two separate picks, two partial pallets shipped.
3
Combined: 40 cases on 2 pallets that could fit on 1. Wasted truck space, double handling cost.
ASOE agent behavior
1
Evaluates all lines together. 23 + 17 = 40 cases. Both SKUs have same case dimensions.
2
Detects mixed-SKU pallet opportunity. 40 cases = 4 full layers on 1 pallet.
3
Checks if customer accepts mixed pallets (most do for same product family).
4
Suggests: ship as 1 mixed pallet. Saves 1 pallet position, reduces freight cost ~$45. Or: round SKU-A to 20, SKU-B to 20 for clean split.
Resolution decision tree
✓
Full layer / full pallet
Order qty mod Ti = 0. No action needed — passes validation automatically.
↑
Auto-round up (within tolerance)
Remainder ≥ 70% of layer. Agent rounds up to next full layer and adjusts PO total. Customer notified, no approval needed if within contract tolerance.
⇅
Propose alternatives
Remainder 30–70% of layer. Agent generates round-up and round-down options with dollar impact, routes to CSR with pre-drafted communication.
!
Broken layer surcharge or exception
Remainder < 30% of layer. Apply surcharge per contract terms, or check customer exception table for waiver. Escalate if no rule covers it.
◈
Cross-line optimization
Multiple broken lines on same PO. Agent checks if combining SKUs onto mixed pallets improves fill rate. Common for same-family products going to same DC.
